# Transit backdrop image (for ChatGPT image generation)

Use: one square image hung at infinity down the course (centered on the vanishing point, rotating with the scenery roll, no parallax, blended as "screen" so black = transparent). The whole level then slowly turns around it, so it should read as a vast radial mechanism, windmill-like only when it turns.

Prompt:

A colossal, undecipherable mechanism seen face-on from enormous distance, centered in a square frame: a huge radial machine of nested rings, spokes, gear-like arms and long blade-like vanes, like a windmill or orrery that nobody could explain, built at the scale of a mountain range. Mostly asymmetrical: arms of unequal length, one ring broken, some vanes missing, structures clustered on one side, no mirror symmetry, no repeating pattern. Austere, monumental, melancholy, awe inspiring; engineering drawn as a ghostly silhouette, not cute, not cartoonish, no characters, no text, no faces, no bright highlights.
Rendering: heavy atmospheric perspective, as if seen through miles of haze. Flat, soft, low-contrast shapes with hard clean edges and a few cel-shaded steps of tone (no gradients inside the shapes, no glossy reflections, no noise or grain). Lit from behind, so it is mostly silhouette with a thin rim of light on the edges.
Palette: dusty violet, plum, indigo, and faint rose-pink, with one pale cream rim light and a tiny touch of teal. Nothing darker than deep indigo, no black inside the structure, no reds.
Background: solid pure black everywhere outside the machine (I will blend it onto a pink sky), with the outer parts of the machine fading softly into black so no edge of the image is visible. Keep a wide empty black margin on all four sides. Square, 4096 x 4096.

Ask for 3 variations; pick the one that is least symmetrical and least like a clock face.
