# Story and world (draft v2, 2026-11-19)

Direction from Jeffry: no child, no humans. The snail is a self-contained house. Melancholy is fine, balanced by the music's high highs. Cute, clay-world look like The Neverhood. Ramps are leylines. Eyestalks grow with distance travelled in the wind. Slightly mystical, Bloodborne-lite but readable (one plain sentence of eight words or fewer, never riddles).

## The idea in one line
A snail is a house it carries. This one is old; its shell is full of rooms it has lived in. It walks out through its own whorls to the place where finished shells become hills.

## Metaphor
- The levels are the **rooms of the shell**, in the order of a life: warm nursery, first leaving, the cellar, the hiding place, the open wind, the long night, the garden.
- **Leylines** are the growth lines of the shell, the rings left behind by every year the snail grew. The snail rides its own history.
- **Wind** is time. It pushes the snail along and it stretches the eyestalks: the farther travelled, the longer the stalks (saved lifetime distance). By the end they trail behind like ribbons.
- Sad part, stated plainly once near the end: **you cannot take the house with you, because the house is you.** The shell stays. Nothing here is scary.

## Plot options
- **A. The Last Whorl (recommended).** The old snail walks outward through its shell to the Garden of Shells. At the end it lies down and the shell becomes a small hill with a door. A tiny new snail wanders in. Bittersweet, a little sad, a little hopeful.
- **B. The Move.** The shell is outgrown. The snail travels through other empty shells looking for a bigger home and ends up carrying the old one on top. Cozy and funny, less sad.
- **C. The Crack.** The shell is breaking. Each level seals a crack with thread. It ends sealed and the snail can never leave: melancholy, quieter, more mysterious.

## Cast (no humans)
- The snail (paper, in a clay world: the only paper thing in the house).
- A **Mender**: an old spider made of clay and thread who darns the shell between runs (the hub, the Attic). Short plain lines, never rules.
- **Echoes**: lantern checkpoints that hold one small clay scene of the snail's past (a first leaf, a rainy night, a snail friend). One caption at most.
- Wallpaper moths, a thumbprint moon, a staircase with too many steps. Odd, never menacing.

## Look and mechanics
- Clay world, paper snail. Stop-motion stutter on scenery (about 12 fps) while the snail stays smooth.
- Eyestalk growth: a saved lifetime-distance counter scales stalk length. Cosmetic only, about 1 hour.
- Echoes at checkpoints (medium), the Attic hub with the Mender (medium), wind gusts as level modifiers (physics risk, later).
- In the editor and menus, "ramp" becomes "leyline".

## Brainstorm threads (2026-11-19, rough, not canon)
Dropped: the Quickfolk and Slowfolk split (too Tolkien), quoted one-liners, and the child story.
- **Who we are:** an archivist snail, old and dry-humoured, not a hero. The job is to re-walk the leylines and check the records against the ground.
- **Leylines:** the ancient paths of the first snails, layered like strata (new slime over old). We retrace steps, we do not discover. Checkpoints are survey stakes. Ghost replays are earlier archivists' survey passes.
- **Central Armature Works:** an implausible snail-steampunk institution, a building the player passes before anything else. Armature also means the wire skeleton inside a clay or stop-motion figure, which fits the clay look. Possible hub in place of the Attic. Brass spiral lifts, slime boilers, shell lathes, pneumatic tubes, a paper room.
- **Earth:** the first snails came down from the sky (links to the existing sky descent, Level 5). Leylines are their landing trails. The old human rooms (kitchen island, stairwell, box fort) can be read as ruins, with no humans shown. Reaching the soil at the end is visiting Earth.
- **Links to existing pieces:** stalk growth is survey fatigue; speed thins the shell; the Garden of Shells is where archivists end; music highs are the moments a first-snail mark is found.
