# Stage 1: The Transit (levels/intro.json, scenery 'mains', build #182, 2026-11-24)

An unfathomably large, melancholy pink space. Mystical and awe-inspiring, not cute. Music: windmill (cut 2:35.0 to 4:59.0), plays straight through. Course: 18 long pieces, all downhill with a cliff dive, bot 74.3 s, generated by tools/gen-intro.js (see "Source of truth" below).

## Look (decided, change only with Jeffry's OK)
- Palette: pink dusk (rose bottom, plum/indigo top; keyframes in gen-intro.js). Listen to the music before choosing colours: burgundy/crimson was tried and rejected as oppressive. Contrast comes from deep violet arches (0x4a3a96), warm cream rails and gates, ramps in periwinkle to lilac (hue 0.66 to 0.78, only for scenery 'mains'), and a few cool complements (teal, aqua, lavender aurora). Cel shading = flat bands of colour with hard steps (the game's 3-step TOON gradient), no smooth gradients inside shapes.
- Fog is light (near 7000, far 62000) so the arches keep their colour. Heavy fog washes everything to pink.
- Structure: huge half-ring arches around the course (rolled per arch so they surround you), thin translucent lilac orrery rings (6) with one soft light each, 8 constellations (stars joined by faint lines), 12 aurora curtains (spread over slots, slanted across the course, cool colours, faint), 3 skeletal leylines (ghost ramps with cross ribs) plus 3 faint single ghost lines, travellers on them, 36 big soft cloud sheets and 40 mist banks (kept few and large on purpose), 40 wind streaks at speed (lilac), dive gates, star points.
- Backdrop machines (img/bd_skel.jpg, bd_ring.png from art/transit_*.png): two huge images at infinity, drawn first (opaque list, custom alpha blend) so they sit behind all scenery. Skeletal sail (outer, vast, hazed, turns -0.022 rad/s) and orbital ring (inner, 70 percent of the sail, turns +0.03 rad/s, haze pockets made transparent by a colour mask). Direction fixed to the course heading, NOT rolling with the scenery. Prompt and notes: docs/transit-backdrop-prompt.md. Windmill-like when they turn.
- The scenery rolls about the course (mainsTheta: 7.85 rad over the level, fastest midway) so there is no up or down. Only scenery turns; ramps and gravity do not.

## Rules learned the hard way
- Never stack many low-alpha translucent sprites: phones show coloured speckle that headless renders do not. Few, large, denser layers, one tint per group, fade near the camera.
- Never make soft sprite textures with a canvas gradient (iOS un-premultiplies alpha on upload: coloured noise). glowTexA is a DataTexture now. If speckle returns, bisect on the phone with `?hide=clouds`, `mist`, `stars`, `bokeh`, `globes`, `aurora`, `backdrop`.
- Objects beyond the camera far plane (90000) are clipped; a big quad's corners count. Keep backdrop corners inside it or draw them first as backdrops.
- Lamps must hang inside the rail tube radius or the depth test hides them; far lights need long distance fades or they vanish.
- Level JSON is fetched with a cache-busting query (stale JSON explained an old palette on the phone).

## Source of truth for the course
tools/gen-intro.js writes levels/intro.json. If Jeffry edits the course in the editor and saves, re-running the generator OVERWRITES those edits. Pick one: (a) editor becomes the source of truth and gen-intro.js is retired (recommended after the first hand edit), or (b) tweaks go into the generator. Never both.
