# Loft (Country Roads)
Long night descent through a loft, built from Level 5's vocabulary (wide 900 landings, 2500 to 3200 drops, hoops, a loop and a swirl) in five scenes: Rafters, Lamp forest (loop), The window (fork), The floor (swirl), Home. Generated by `node tools/gen-loft.js`. Music `countryroads` (track 4, 77.5 BPM, cut by tools/cut-music.py), good bot about 115 s.
- **Two routes at the fork** (piece 18): A, the lamp road, a switchback (40, 80, 40 degree arcs, radius 4500) with two climbs that bleed speed: long and safe. B, a leap from piece 18 straight to piece 24 (about 19700 u, drop 5300): needs about 2400+ u/s and a straight aim. `node tools/test-fork.js levels/loft.json 18 24` flies arcs in the real game: exact aim lands from 2200 to 3000 u/s, 1800 does not.
- **Scenery `loft`** (index.html, `buildLoft`): wood floor, 6 furniture sprites standing on it where the course comes down near it, pendant lights (cord, clay shade, glow) beside every other ramp, two windows with the night city in three parallax layers (at the fork and past the finish), no hills or clouds. Art: art/web/loft_* from art/loft (window and city layers keyed to transparent).
- Not built: ceiling beams, ramp texture from the clay set, wall, moonlight beam, small props.
